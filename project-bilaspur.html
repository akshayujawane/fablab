AIIMS Bilaspur — Super-Specialty Hospital

EPC • Multi product facility • India-Bilaspur • 750 Bedded

Project Overview

AIIMS Bilaspur, a 750-bedded super-specialty hospital with 15–20 super-specialty departments, is designed and engineered for high-precision infection control, reliable medical utilities, and uninterrupted healthcare operations.

Key Highlights & Scope

  • 750-bedded tertiary healthcare institution with 15–20 super-specialty departments
  • High-efficiency HVAC, dedicated AHUs, and HEPA-filtered sterile zones
  • Complete HT/LT electrical panels, power backup, and building automation
  • Integrated medical equipment areas, clean corridors, and NABH/healthcare compliance
