Injectable Insulin Facility — Sri Lanka

Turnkey • Injectable • Sri Lanka • 10,00,000 sq. ft.

Project Overview

Fablab spearheaded the development of Sri Lanka's first insulin manufacturing facility—a landmark 10,00,000 sq. ft. turnkey sterile injectable and biologics project driving pharmaceutical self-reliance in the nation.

Key Highlights & Scope

  • Project Location: Sri Lanka — Nation's first Insulin Manufacturing Facility
  • FabLab's Role: Full EPC & Turnkey support with process design & equipment selection
  • Core Expertise: Biologics & fermentation-based API production
  • Technology Transfer: Seamless integration ensuring global quality benchmarks
  • Impact: Major step towards pharmaceutical self-reliance in Sri Lanka
