Journey of Excellence: 2013 – 2024

Since our inception in 2013, Fablab has continuously evolved—expanding beyond HVAC into global EPC markets, cleanroom manufacturing, and pharmaceutical machinery.

Over a Decade of Innovation

Every year reflects our commitment to high standards, strategic expansion, and engineering breakthroughs that redefine excellence worldwide.

2024

Global Expansion & Industry Benchmarks

A year marked by strategic global expansion and groundbreaking advancements. We continue to strengthen our presence worldwide, setting new industry standards.

2023

New Factory for Europe & Southeast Asia

Established a new manufacturing factory to support operations in Europe and Southeast Asia, further strengthening our international presence.

2022

Germany Office & Healthcare Turnkey Alliance

Expanded into Southeast Asia with a strategic office in Germany, and formed a key partnership with Technecon for turnkey healthcare projects.

2021

Pan-India Regional Footprint

Strengthened regional presence with new offices in South India, Chandigarh, and Ahmedabad, solidifying the company's national footprint.

2020

IV Projects & First Biopharma Plant in Sri Lanka

Diversified services by establishing a new company focused on IV projects and securing the first biopharma project in Sri Lanka.

2019

Major 4000TR HVAC Contract & Office Expansion

Expanded infrastructure with an additional 1,500 sq. ft. office space and secured a major 4,000TR air conditioning contract.

2018

Launch of Fabfill Machinery

Founded Fabfill, delivering innovative solutions for pharmaceutical filling and packing machines, contributing to industry advancement.

2017

Dedicated Pharmaceutical Machinery Division

Launched a dedicated division for pharmaceutical machinery, expanding service offerings in the pharmaceutical sector.

2016

International Markets & Cleanroom Panel Manufacturing

Purchased a 1,500 sq. ft. office, entered international markets, and began in-house cleanroom panel manufacturing.

2015

Electrical & Automation Division

Strengthened capabilities by launching a dedicated electrical and automation division, along with securing a prestigious 1,700TR air conditioning contract.

2014

Multi-Sector Expansion

Expanded operations to include commercial, industrial, and institutional sectors, securing high-value contracts and bolstering the company's portfolio.

2013

The Foundation

Fablab was founded as a specialized HVAC contracting firm, earning early recognition for excellence in the industry.
