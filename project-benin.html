Multi-Product Facility — Benin

EPC • Multi product facility • Benin • 2,24,000 sq. ft.

Project Overview

Fablab successfully delivered EPC services for a multi-product pharmaceutical facility in Benin, comprising four specialized buildings. The facility is designed to manufacture over 50 General products, 20+ IV products, 4+ Oncology products, 10+ Beta-lactam products, and 4 API products. Our scope included comprehensive technology transfer, enabling seamless production and adherence to global quality standards.

Key Highlights & Scope

  • 4 specialized pharmaceutical manufacturing buildings across 2,24,000 sq. ft.
  • 50+ General formulation products & 20+ IV sterile products
  • 4+ dedicated Oncology suites & 10+ Beta-lactam segregated production lines
  • 4 Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient (API) manufacturing units
  • Complete EPC delivery including comprehensive technology transfer & global GMP compliance
